Privacy Policy for John In Space

Last updated: August 2, 2026

John In Space does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Data and tracking

The game does not include advertising, tracking technologies, third-party analytics, or an app-specific user account system.

Local game data

Game progress, unlocked levels, settings, and purchase status are stored locally on the device. Apple may also retain purchase entitlements so that a previous non-consumable purchase can be restored.

Apple Game Center

John In Space uses Apple Game Center for authentication and leaderboards. High scores, highest reached level, and Hall of Fame completion may be submitted to Game Center when the player is signed in.

In-App Purchase

John In Space uses Apple StoreKit for the optional one-time purchase that unlocks Levels 16–45. Payment details and purchase history are processed by Apple. The developer does not receive or store payment-card information.

Network access

The game remains playable without Game Center sign-in. Network access is only needed for Apple services such as Game Center, purchasing, and restoring purchases.

Apple services

Apple's handling of Game Center and App Store information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.
