Helpful information

When contacting support, please include:

  • Your iPhone model
  • Your iOS version
  • The John In Space app version
  • The level where the issue occurred
  • A short description of the problem

Restore a purchase

John In Space includes one optional, non-consumable purchase that unlocks Levels 16–45. To restore a previous purchase, open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchase.
